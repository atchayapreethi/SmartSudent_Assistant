Student Support FAQ

Q: How can I apply for leave?
A: Students can apply for leave through the college leave application process.

Q: How can I check my attendance?
A: Students can check their attendance through the college student portal.

Q: How can I contact the college office?
A: Students can contact the college office during official working hours.

Q: What should I do if I have an academic problem?
A: Students should contact their class advisor or academic support staff.

Q: How can I get information about examinations?
A: Students can check official examination notices and contact the examination cell.
